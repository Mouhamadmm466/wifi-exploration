## How to read the table

“Selected full text” means the methods, results, or other named sections in the JSON record were inspected. “Abstract/excerpts” means full methods were not checked. “Technical sections” covers software, standards, or vendor documentation. Dates for living documents are left unspecified when no publication date was verified.

- `R13`: the primary paper verifies the 2026 ICOIN work. The exact publication date remains unverified. Its DOI was checked only against a secondary index, so the BibTeX deliberately omits that DOI until a primary publisher record is verified. The original candidate identifier and its caveat remain in the JSON record.
- `M05`: the bibliography cites the institutional report that was read, dated 2002. The related 2003 journal DOI is not attached to the 2002 report entry.
- `M08`: the bibliography cites the 2023 author preprint that was inspected and notes the 2024 journal version. These are one research work.
- `R04` and `R12` are workshop/preprint records; their workshop association is not main-track ICML or NeurIPS acceptance. `R11` has no verified peer-reviewed venue.
- `M07` is a December 2024 conference paper indexed by IEEE in March 2025. The indexing date is not the conference date.
- `M03` and `M04` are moving tool documentation. Their claims apply to the sections inspected on the review date, not every release or external extension. `M12` is a dated W3C Candidate Recommendation Draft, not a final Recommendation.
